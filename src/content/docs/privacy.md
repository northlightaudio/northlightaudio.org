This website counts visits so we can see which pages people read and which links
brought them here. It doesn't use cookies, it doesn't store anything in your browser,
and it doesn't follow you to other sites.

The Northlight application is separate, and it sends nothing about you or your work
anywhere, as [the pledge](/pledge/) promises.

## What the site counts

The counting is done by [GoatCounter](https://www.goatcounter.com), an open source
analytics service that keeps totals rather than a record of each visitor. For each page
view it keeps:

- the page, and the site or link you came from;
- the campaign and source in the link's address, when the link has them (for example
  `utm_campaign=launch`), so we know which posts and ads bring people here;
- your browser and operating system, worked out from what your browser reports;
- your screen width, your browser's language and your country.

It also counts clicks on the download links, under the campaign you arrived from.

## What it doesn't keep

GoatCounter doesn't store your IP address or the full description your browser sends
of itself. To avoid counting one visit twice, it briefly matches the IP address and
browser to a random number in memory, for up to eight hours, and then forgets it. Its
[privacy policy](https://www.goatcounter.com/help/privacy) has the details.

Nothing on this site sets cookies or uses your browser's storage, so there is nothing
to accept or clear. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may keep its own
server logs; [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement)
covers those.
