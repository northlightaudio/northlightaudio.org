## About Northlight

A short description:

> Northlight is a free and open source digital audio workstation for Windows, for
> recording, arranging and mixing music. It's released under the GNU AGPLv3 and made in
> the open on GitHub.

A longer one:

> Northlight is a free and open source digital audio workstation for Windows. It records
> many tracks at once at low latency over ASIO, hosts VST3 and CLAP plugins, and comes
> with its own instruments: Pulsar, a drum synth; Aurora, for basses and keys; Nebula,
> for pads; Starbit, a chiptune synth; and a sampler. Every track has a channel strip
> with a gate, EQ, compressor, de-esser, saturation and limiter, and sung parts can be
> pitch-corrected note by note. Anyone can use Northlight for anything, and its source
> code is public under the GNU AGPLv3. It hasn't had its first release yet.

### Using the logo

- Leave clear space around the mark at least as wide as the moon on every side.
- Don't use the mark smaller than 16 px, or the lockup narrower than 96 px.
- The mark always comes before the name, and the name sits on the horizon line.
- Don't recolour the moon blue, outline it, make it a full circle, add a gradient or a
  glow, or rotate the mark. Don't set the name in another typeface.
- On a photograph, use the one-colour version in the moon's cream (`#F1EEDF`), so the horizon
  doesn't disappear into the sky.

## Colours

Northlight looks like a Hebridean island in early evening: dark peat and turf greens, an
overcast blue sky and a cream moon. The blue marks anything active, and red is kept for
recording.
