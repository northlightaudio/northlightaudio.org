Music belongs to everyone. So should the tools for making it.

Northlight exists because we think the means to record, shape and share music should be
open to anyone who wants them, and should stay that way. This page sets out what we
believe and what we promise. Hold us to it.

## What we believe

**Anyone should be able to make music.** Recording a song, scoring a short film, mixing a
friend's band: none of it should depend on what you earn, where you live, or whether a
company still sells the software you learned on. A computer and the wish to make
something should be enough.

**The tools you depend on should belong to you.** When the software that holds your work
can have its price raised, its features locked or its servers switched off, your work is
never quite yours. Free software can't be taken back.

**How a tool works is part of the craft.** In Northlight, a compressor, a time-stretch or
a mixer is code that anyone can read. Someone curious about how audio software works can
learn from it, and someone who finds a fault can see why it happens and fix it.

**Good tools come from people working in the open.** The people who use Northlight know
best what it needs. Their bug reports, ideas, documentation and code shape it, and every
decision is made where they can see it.

## What we promise

1. **Northlight will always be free of charge.** No price, no subscription, no paid "pro"
   edition and no features held back for people who pay.
2. **You will never need an account.** Northlight works offline. It will never ask you to
   sign in, activate a licence or check in with a server before you can use it.
3. **What you make is yours.** Northlight claims nothing over your music. Release it, sell
   it or give it away, and you owe us nothing, not even a credit.
4. **Your privacy is respected.** Northlight sends nothing about you or your work
   anywhere. If we ever add crash reports, usage statistics or update checks, they will
   be off until you turn them on, and we will say exactly what they send.
5. **Your projects stay readable.** Projects are saved as plain text that any program can
   open, and your recordings as ordinary audio files. You will never need Northlight's
   permission to get at your own work.
6. **The code stays open.** Every release is free software under the GNU AGPLv3. Anyone
   who receives Northlight is entitled to its source code, and contributors keep the
   copyright in what they write, so nobody, including us, can turn it into a closed
   product.
7. **We build it for everyone.** Every part of Northlight should work from the keyboard
   and with a screen reader, in high contrast and at any display scale. Where it falls
   short, that is a bug, and we treat it as one.
8. **We work in the open.** Plans, bug reports and discussions are public. When we make a
   decision, we write down why.

## Where we are today

Northlight runs only on Windows for now. The code is written so it can be ported, but
until it is, that is a limit on who can use it, and we say so plainly. It also hasn't had
its first release yet: you can [build it yourself](/download/#build-it-yourself) today,
and installers will follow.

## What we ask of you

Use it, and make something. Tell us what's broken and what's missing. Help someone else
get started. If you can, improve the manual or the code. The pledge is ours to keep, and
Northlight is yours to shape. [Here's how to join in](/contributing/).
