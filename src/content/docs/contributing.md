---
title: Contributing
description: How to report bugs, suggest ideas and contribute code.
---

- **Bugs:** open an [issue](https://github.com/northlightaudio/northlight/issues) with the
  bug report form.
- **Ideas and questions:** start a
  [discussion](https://github.com/northlightaudio/northlight/discussions).
- **Code and documentation:** read the
  [contributing guide](https://github.com/northlightaudio/.github/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md)
  and open a pull request.

Everyone taking part follows the
[code of conduct](https://github.com/northlightaudio/.github/blob/main/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
