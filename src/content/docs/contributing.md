Northlight is made by the people who use it. You don't need to write code to help, and
nobody needs permission to start.

## Ways to help

- **Use it and tell us what happens.** A clear bug report is one of the most useful
  things anyone can give. Open an
  [issue](https://github.com/northlightaudio/northlight/issues) with the bug report form.
- **Share ideas and ask questions.** Start a
  [discussion](https://github.com/northlightaudio/northlight/discussions). Questions show
  us where Northlight or its manual isn't clear yet.
- **Improve the manual.** If something confused you, you're the right person to explain
  it. The manual lives in the
  [northlight repository](https://github.com/northlightaudio/northlight) beside the code.
- **Write code.** Read the
  [contributing guide](https://github.com/northlightaudio/.github/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md)
  and open a pull request. You keep the copyright in what you write, and it's shared
  under the same [licence](/licence/) as the rest of Northlight.
- **Help someone else.** Answer a question, show a friend how to record their first song,
  or tell people Northlight exists.

## How we work together

Everyone taking part follows the
[code of conduct](https://github.com/northlightaudio/.github/blob/main/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
Plans and decisions are made in public, on GitHub, so anyone can see what's happening and
why. That's part of [the pledge](/pledge/).
