to try and to record with, with rough edges still to find. The

## System requirements

<div class="nl-rows">
	<div>
		<strong>Windows</strong>
		<p>Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. There's no version for Mac or Linux yet.</p>
	</div>
	<div>
		<strong>Audio</strong>
		<p>An audio interface with an ASIO driver is recommended. Windows Audio (WASAPI) works too, including with a computer's built-in sound.</p>
	</div>
	<div>
		<strong>Plugins</strong>
		<p>Optional. Northlight comes with its own instruments and effects, and runs VST3 and CLAP plugins when you have them.</p>
	</div>
</div>

## Build it yourself

The source is on [GitHub](https://github.com/northlightaudio/northlight). Building needs
Visual Studio 2022 or later with the "Desktop development with C++" workload, and Git.
Visual Studio Community is free for individuals and open source work. The repository's
README has the steps. ASIO support is built in, with nothing extra to download.
