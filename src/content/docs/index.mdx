<p class="nl-eyebrow">Why Northlight</p>

## The freedom to make music

Recording a song used to need a studio. Now it needs a computer and the right software,
and the right software usually comes with a price, a subscription, or an account that can
be closed. Whether you get to make music shouldn't depend on any of that.

Northlight is a complete studio that costs nothing and belongs to the people who use it.

<CardGrid>
	<Card title="Free for everyone" icon="heart">

		downloaded it, you can keep it.

	<Card title="For any purpose" icon="seti:audio">

## Free, not cut down

Northlight is meant for finished records. You can record a band at low latency, comp
the best takes, mix the song and export the master without leaving it.

<div class="nl-feature nl-feature--stacked">
	<div>
		### Vocal pitch editing

		Northlight finds every note in a sung part. Move a note, split or join notes, or
		correct the whole take towards the key, and quieten the breaths between phrases.
		A correction moves a note's centre but keeps the singer's vibrato and the slides
		between notes, so it still sounds like them. You can change or undo any edit
		later.
	</div>
	<Screenshot
		src={pitch}
		alt="The pitch editor: a vocal take's notes drawn as blocks on a piano roll, with the sung pitch traced through each one and two notes labelled with how far off the key they are in cents."
		caption="Corrected towards A major."
	/>
</div>

<div class="nl-feature">
	<div>
		### Instruments to start with

		You don't need to buy anything to start writing. Northlight comes with Pulsar, a
		drum synth; Aurora, for basses and keys; Nebula, for slow, drifting pads; Starbit,
		a chiptune synth; Lumen, an electric piano; Cirrus, an FM synth; and a sampler for
		your own sounds. When you want more, it runs your VST3 and CLAP plugins too.
	</div>
	<Screenshot
		src={pulsar}
		alt="Pulsar, Northlight's drum synth: eight pads with their sounds drawn as waveforms, and controls for the kick."

</div>

<div class="nl-feature nl-feature--stacked">

		### An electric piano and an FM synth

		Lumen is a modelled electric piano. Each note is a hammer striking a tine, so playing
		harder makes it brighter as well as louder, and it has its own tremolo, phaser and
		chorus. Cirrus is a four-operator FM synth, for electric pianos, bells, basses, brass
		and organ. Its display shows what the FM is doing to the note you're playing.
	</div>

<div class="nl-feature nl-feature--flipped">
	<div>
		### A mixer that keeps up

		Send to reverb and echo on FX tracks, group tracks into buses, and automate
		anything. Plugin delays are compensated, so everything stays in time. When the mix is done, export to WAV, AIFF, FLAC or Ogg,
		with loudness normalisation.
	</div>

		Every track and bus has a gate, EQ, compressor, de-esser, saturation and limiter
		built in, ready to switch on. Each one shows what it's doing as you play: the EQ
		curve, the compressor's gain reduction over time, the saturation curve. Drag them
		into any order, or start from a preset for vocals, guitars, drums or the mix bus.

## Effects with a character of their own

Besides the everyday EQs, compressors, reverbs and delays, Northlight has seven effects
with faces of their own. They show you what they're doing to the sound as it plays.

<div class="nl-effects">
	<Effect
		name="Glue Compressor"
		kind="Bus compressor"
		use="Pulls a mix, or a group of drums or vocals, together."
		src={glue}
		alt="The Glue Compressor: a ribbon of the last three seconds of the mix going in and coming out, a gain reduction arc, and stepped Ratio, Attack and Release selectors."
	>
		Stepped controls, as on a mixing console, and an Auto release that recovers quickly from
		a hit and slowly from held compression. Mix blends in parallel compression. Instead of a
		transfer curve, its display shows how far it pulls the peaks down towards the body of the
		sound, so you can see how much punch you're keeping.
	</Effect>
	<Effect
		name="Orbit"
		kind="Tape machine"
		use="Warmth and soft compression for a bus or the master."
		src={orbit}
		alt="Orbit, a studio reel-to-reel tape machine: two metal reels, a tape time counter, VU meters and controls for speed, EQ, level and tape type."
	>
		The tape itself is modelled, so loud passages compress softly and the highs saturate first.
		Choose the tape speed, the type of tape and how hard you hit it, and add hiss and flutter
		if you want them.
	</Effect>
	<Effect
		name="Patina"
		kind="Vintage colour"
		use="Makes a sound old, from a little warmth to falling apart."
		src={patina}
		alt="Patina, a weathered steel rack unit with six stages, Heat, Chamber, Drift, Grain, Wear and Crush, each with an amber display, and a large Age knob from New to Ruined."
	>
		Six stages age a sound in different ways: drive, a room, wow and flutter, crackle and hiss,
		worn-out highs, and fewer bits. One Age knob takes all of it from new to ruined.
	</Effect>
	<Effect
		name="Tape Echo"
		kind="Tape delay"
		use="Gives a part warm, wobbling echoes that can swell into feedback."
		src={tapeEcho}
		alt="Tape Echo, a cream and green 1970s-style unit with a tape loop running past a record head and four playback heads, a VU meter, and large Heads, Repeat Rate, Intensity and Echo Volume knobs."
	>
		A tape loop with four playback heads and a spring reverb. Turn the feedback past 100% and
		it runs away into oscillation. Change the repeat rate and the echoes bend in pitch as the
		motor settles.
	</Effect>
	<Effect
		name="Bloom"
		kind="Effect reverb"
		use="Reverb as an effect, in spaces far bigger than any real room."
		src={bloom}
		alt="Bloom: a round display of the reverb tail as rings, with a Freeze button in the middle, and controls for Decay, Size, Shimmer and Swell."
	>
		Tails last up to a minute. Freeze holds one for good, Shimmer makes it climb in pitch as
		it rings, and Swell fades it in like a reversed reverb. Gate cuts it short and Duck tucks
		it under the dry sound.
	</Effect>
	<Effect
		name="Vesper"
		kind="Vocal reverb"
		use="Gives a vocal space without clouding the words."
		src={vesper}
		alt="Vesper: a dusk-coloured reverb with lights for Singing, Kept Out and Line End, a display of the tail against the voice, and controls for Decay, Clarity, Duck and Ends."
	>
		Vesper listens to the singing. It keeps consonants, sibilance and breaths out of the
		reverb, lowers the tail while the singer sings, and lifts it at the end of each line.
		Catch holds the tail as a pad under the next phrase.
	</Effect>
	<Effect
		name="Corona"
		kind="Guitar amp"
		use="Plays a guitar or bass through a classic two-twelve combo."
		src={corona}
		alt="Corona, a tube guitar amp with a gold control panel for preamp, reverb, tremolo and power, a woven grille cloth and a Corona badge."
	>
		Modelled stage by stage on a 1960s American combo with spring reverb and tremolo, from the
		first tube to the speaker. On its cabinet page, choose one of five cabinets or load your
		own impulse response, and place two mics on the speaker.
	</Effect>
</div>

<div class="nl-feature nl-feature--stacked">
	<div>
		### Guitar pedals

		Put Corona at the end of a pedalboard. Northlight has five stompboxes for guitar and bass:
		Polaris, a tuner; Gravity, a compressor; Flare, a distortion; Tide, a chorus; and Comet,
		an analog delay. Each has only the few knobs a pedal has, and none adds latency, so you
		can play through them live.
	</div>
